Where this sits in the teleoperation loop

The operator keeps control of the robot. This layer handles the reading, record-keeping and paperwork that would otherwise fall to them after the walkdown.

Operator

Drives Roger to each stop

From the safe area, through the headset, as today.

What this demo does not prove yet

Real footage

All frames are rendered, so every read has an exact answer key. The next step is validating on real head-camera frames from a pilot site.

Gauge variety

One dial style is modelled. Real platforms mix dial faces, digital displays, sight glasses and valve positions.

Finding the gauge

Here each frame is already centred on its gauge. In the field, a detector picks the gauge out of the wider camera view and matches it to its asset tag.

Motion blur

The weakest condition: on held-out frames it misses the 99% target. The practical fix is having Roger hold still and re-shoot when confidence is low.
