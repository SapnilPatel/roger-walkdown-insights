Gauge variety

One dial style is modelled. Platforms also have digital displays, sight glasses and valve positions.

Finding the gauge

Frames here are centred on the gauge. In the field a detector picks it out of the wider view and matches it to its asset tag.

Motion blur

The weakest condition, below the 99% target on held-out frames. The practical fix: hold still and re-shoot when confidence is low.
